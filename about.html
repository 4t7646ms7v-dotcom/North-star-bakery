About North Star Bakery

Our Story

Here at North Star we are a family-owned establishment, and we have been serving the people here in Ormond Beach for over 30 years. We pride ourselves on only using the best and freshest ingredients for our customers.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

At North Star Bakery, we prioritize our products as our main focus and believe that our success lies in improving the quality and taste of our products.
